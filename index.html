S & A Games

Three browser games, built from scratch. No install, no build step, no dependencies — pick one and play.

Physics mini-golf

Gravity Golf

Drag back, release, and let gravity do the rest.

  • 9 hand-made holes across three sections
  • Planets pull, suns burn, repulsors push
  • Progress and scorecard saved in your browser

Play Gravity Golf

Tower defense

Castle Defense

Garrison your turrets and hold the gate for a hundred waves.

  • Four tower types, each upgradable three levels
  • A boss every tenth wave, escalating to wave 100
  • Chunky pixel-art rendering, drawn low and scaled up

Play Castle Defense

Turn-based strategy

Pentakeover

Five unit types, five factions, five citadels to hold.

  • Procedural territory map, different every seed
  • Counter triangle, terrain and supply lines to work around
  • Play the computer at three levels, or hotseat a friend

Play Pentakeover
