S & A Games

Five browser games, built from scratch. No install, no build step, no dependencies — pick one and play.

Physics mini-golf

Gravity Golf

Drag back, release, and let gravity do the rest.

  • 15 hand-made holes across three sections
  • Planets pull, suns burn, repulsors push
  • Progress and scorecard saved in your browser

Play Gravity Golf

Tower defense

Castle Defense

Garrison your turrets and hold the gate for a hundred waves.

  • Four tower types, each upgradable three levels
  • A boss every tenth wave, escalating to wave 100
  • Chunky pixel-art rendering, drawn low and scaled up

Play Castle Defense

Turn-based strategy

Pentakeover

Five unit types, five factions, five citadels to hold.

  • Procedural territory map, different every seed
  • Counter triangle, terrain and supply lines to work around
  • Play the computer at three levels, or hotseat a friend

Play Pentakeover

Two-player artillery

Tank Duel

Two tanks, one hill, and one keyboard between you.

  • Set an angle and a power, then watch it arc
  • Wind shifts every turn and the ground craters where you miss
  • The one game here you cannot play alone

Play Tank Duel

Cave roguelite platformer

Delve

Five cave depths, one lantern and one life.

  • Winding tunnels and tall shafts, rerolled every run
  • Mouse-aimed shots, seven crystal powerups and run relics
  • A Warden waiting at the bottom of Depth 5

Play Delve
